Research paper · 2026-05-18

Online Conformal Prediction for Non-Exchangeable Panel Data

Daohong Tu, Kay Giesecke · arXiv:2605.17705

One of the paper's three real-data panels is built from HF Data Library clean U.S. equity data, Clean version, Hourly timeframe. The HF panel covers the second half of 2024 (2024-07-02 to 2024-12-31), targets next-hour return, and splits 495 start-aligned tickers into 395 calibration and 100 test tickers over a 500-hour horizon.

For the HF panel, we use the HF Data Library clean U.S. equity data, selecting the Clean version and Hourly timeframe from the download/API portal https://hfdatalibrary.com/pages/download.

Product · 2026

TradeMood

Urticad Tech Ltd · iOS / iPadOS / watchOS app

A commercial trading-readiness app. Its market-complexity service is built on historical SPY data from the HF Data Library and served to the app through the vendor's own endpoint (api.urticad.com/api/v1/complexity/mkt); the app itself sends only the ticker symbol. The privacy policy carries the CC BY attribution and passes through the library's own post-March-2022 IEX caveat.

The market service uses historical SPY data from HF Data Library, cited as Elkassabgi, A. (2026), HF Data Library: Free 1-Minute Intraday U.S. Equity Data, Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.19501605
